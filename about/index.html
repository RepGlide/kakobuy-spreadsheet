About

Last updated: September 4, 2026

What this resource is

KakoBuy Spreadsheet is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a shared KakoBuy spreadsheet. It links to product categories and to a fixed Google Sheet, and it explains a question-led research method — the Question Ladder — for separating what a page shows from what is still missing and what must be confirmed elsewhere.

What this resource is not

This website is not the official KakoBuy site, a product library, a store, a QC certification, a shopping agent, an order tracker, a fee calculator, or a purchase guarantee. It does not save user input, and it does not generate personalized results.

Independence

This resource is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by KakoBuy or Google. Any reference to KakoBuy is descriptive, used to identify the spreadsheet topic this guide discusses.

No verification or guarantee

Nothing on this site verifies a product, certifies quality, or guarantees an outcome. Seeing a price, image, option name, QC label, or shipping claim on a linked page is not proof of anything. The question method exists to keep those signals in their place.

How to use it

Open the sheet, browse a category, and ask one question at a time. Mark each fact as Known, Unknown, or Not knowable from the page, and pause when the next click cannot answer your question.
